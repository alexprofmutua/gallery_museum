# Cinematic room direction

- The Enchanted Corridor takes its purple and gold palette, arched bays, and fairy-tale atmosphere from the *Sofia the First* reference. Each bay brightens and fills with golden motes as you approach.
- Our Little Cinema is almost black, with low aisle lighting and a painted star field overhead. A faint violet and blue nebula crosses the ceiling.
- The Forever Room is a brass and glass conservatory under a night sky. White and blush roses flank the marble pedestal. Three inscriptions from *The Originals* preserve the Mikaelson reference.

The room geometry and artwork are original interpretations of those references.
