# Entrance and gallery asset credits

## Architectural materials

The entrance and gallery use local WebP copies of three Poly Haven textures. The diffuse maps are 1K and the two optional floor normal maps are 512 pixels square. The source assets use CC0, which does not require attribution. The links below retain provenance. [Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license)

- Checkered stone floor in the entrance. [Floor Tiles 06](https://polyhaven.com/a/floor_tiles_06)
- Herringbone floor in the gallery. [Herringbone Parquet](https://polyhaven.com/a/herringbone_parquet)
- Veining on the columns. [White Marble](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_marble)

The room geometry, furniture, frames, shadow decals, and remaining illustrations were authored for this project. The app serves the assets locally.
